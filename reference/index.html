Who we are

A Saudi health technology company with a global outlook

Twins Health is the healthcare company of Obeikan Investment Group. We help healthcare organizations go digital, through our own products and deep transformation expertise.

Our flagship platform, Saned Health, brings medical, mental and social care onto one cloud system shared by providers, patients and payers.

About Twins Health

Our vision

To be the leading digital health transformation partner from Saudi Arabia to the world, building connected, value-based healthcare systems where every patient receives coordinated care.

Our mission

To transform healthcare delivery through practical, intelligent digital platforms that integrate medical, mental and social care, connect providers, patients and payers, and deliver measurable outcomes.

Saned Health platform

Every patient, every team, one view

Care teams see risk, care plans and outcomes in one place. Patients follow their own plan in the Beneficiary App. Everything runs on one shared record, integrated with NPHIES.

  • Care plans tracked from hospital to home
  • Patients engaged through their own app
  • Outcomes measured for providers and payers
